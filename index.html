How to play

Easy to learn, hard to put down

  1. 1

    Tap

    Tap a tile that is free on top and on at least one side.

  2. 2

    Match

    It moves to the tray. Two matching tiles in the tray disappear.

  3. 3

    Clear

    Clear the board before the tray fills up. Play fast for more stars.

Your island

Every level grows your world

From a little hut to a castle

Spend the stars you earn on your home and garden. Upgrade the house, the tree, the fountain, the rest area, the pool and the barn, all in 3D, and watch them change.

An island that never ends

Each chapter opens a new region to restore: the Mill, the Riverside, the Marketplace and the Forest Camp, then on to new rounds. The map keeps growing as long as you keep playing.

Your garden and farm

Plant wheat and strawberries in eight fields, raise chickens, lambs, calves and cows in the barn, and collect gems from your buildings every day.

Boss Levels and chests

Every sixth level is a Boss Level with a tougher board and a special chest. Star chests, region chests, a daily reward and a weekly event keep the surprises coming.

FAQ

Questions

Is Yamegu free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. It shows ads, and some optional rewards come from watching a short video. There are no in-app purchases.

Which devices does it run on?

Android devices running Android 7.0 or newer. An iPhone version is not available yet.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Levels, the island and your progress all work without a connection. Ads and video rewards need the internet.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your progress is saved on your device, so uninstalling the game deletes it.

Is there a time limit?

No. The clock only affects how many stars you earn. Take your time and you will still finish every level with at least one star.

Which languages are supported?

English, Turkish, German, Spanish, Russian and Simplified Chinese.

What data does the game collect?

Ads (Google AdMob) and analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) use your device's advertising ID and basic device information. The details are in our privacy policy.

How do I contact you?

Email us at support@gumusellabs.com. We read every message.
