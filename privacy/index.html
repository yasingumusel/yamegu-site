Privacy Policy

Yamegu: Island Mahjong

Effective date: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Yamegu: Island Mahjong mobile game ("the game") processes. Developer: Gumusel Labs (Yasin Gümüşel). Contact: support@gumusellabs.com.

Data we collect

The game does not require an account and does not collect your real name, email address or contacts. Your game progress (level, gold, settings) is stored on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. If you choose to join the weekly leaderboard, a small part of it is sent to our servers, as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show ads. To serve and measure ads and prevent abuse, Google may collect: the advertising ID, device and app information (model, operating system, language), IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions and diagnostics. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partners.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to improve it, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records gameplay events (levels started, completed or failed, stars earned, upgrades, which ad placements were used) together with an app instance ID, the advertising ID, device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and the country derived from the IP address. These events are not linked to your name or any account. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Weekly leaderboard (Google Cloud Firestore)

Joining the weekly leaderboard is optional. On first launch the game asks you to choose a nickname; you can skip this. If you choose a nickname, the game sends the following to Google Cloud Firestore (a Firebase service with servers in the European Union) and keeps it there: your nickname, your weekly event points, the highest level you have reached, the time of the last update, and a random anonymous ID that Firebase Authentication creates for your installation. Your nickname, points and level are visible to all players on the leaderboard. Do not use your real name or other personal details as a nickname.

Nicknames are checked for offensive words. Players can report a nickname as offensive or report suspected cheating. A report contains the reported nickname and its anonymous ID, the week, the reason and the reporter's anonymous ID, and is visible only to us. We review reports and may remove nicknames and scores or block an ID from the leaderboard.

You choose your nickname once; you can change it one time later in Settings (this costs in-game gems). Each nickname can belong to only one player, so we also store a record linking your nickname to your anonymous ID. To have all of your leaderboard data deleted, email us your nickname and we will delete it within 30 days. If you never choose a nickname, nothing is sent.

Consent and your choices

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form on first launch and may accept or decline personalised ads; analytics follows the same choice. All players can reset or delete their advertising ID under device Settings > Google > Ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Permissions

Internet access (for ads and analytics) and vibration (for touch feedback).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: support@gumusellabs.com.
